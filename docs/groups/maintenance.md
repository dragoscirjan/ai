# Coordination and maintenance

Coordinate agents, repair instruction routes, and improve the working process.

Choose by the task you have now. These groups describe reader needs, not a mandatory delivery pipeline.

## [`subagent-orchestration`](/skills/subagent-orchestration)

Before delegating, bound child-agent work, assign read/write lanes, integrate results, review, validate, and clean temporary lanes.

**Choose it when:** The operator has authorized bounded specialist, independent-review, or parallel work.

## [`cleanup-workspaces`](/skills/cleanup-workspaces)

Audit integrated Git/Jujutsu workspaces, stale registrations, or authorized temporary lanes; apply only an approved exact cleanup plan.

**Choose it when:** You want to audit integrated workspaces, stale registrations, or explicitly authorized temporary lanes.

## [`instruction-maintenance`](/skills/instruction-maintenance)

Audit instruction drift, routing, and skill metadata; propose exact changes for human approval before mutation.

**Choose it when:** Instruction routing, dependencies, metadata, or policy documents appear missing, stale, conflicting, or unsuitable.

## [`retro`](/skills/retro)

Review a bounded work period for evidence-backed process improvements, including workflow, instructions, tools, and handoffs.

**Choose it when:** A bounded completed work period has enough evidence to review what helped, failed, or should change.

## Try a workflow

- [Delegate only the work that was authorized](/workflows/delegate-bounded-work) — The operator explicitly asks for specialists, independent review, or parallel work.
- [Coordinate independent review without writers](/workflows/coordinate-read-only-review) — You want independent assessment but all changes must stay with the parent.
- [Clean only proven, approved workspaces](/workflows/clean-integrated-workspaces) — Completed work may have left integrated workspaces or stale registrations.
- [Repair instruction drift through exact approval](/workflows/repair-instruction-routes) — Routes, requirements, or policy instructions are missing, duplicated, stale, or conflicting.
- [Turn a completed issue into a small improvement](/workflows/learn-from-completed-work) — A bounded work period has enough evidence to examine the process.
