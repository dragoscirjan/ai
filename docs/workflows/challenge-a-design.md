# Challenge a plan without implementing it

This is a focused procedure. For its place in a larger workflow, see [Plan a project or feature](/workflows/plan-a-project).

A confident proposal may conceal unresolved assumptions or decisions.

**Finish with:** Sharper decisions, unresolved risks, and evidence needed to choose the next step.

## Before you start

Use a trusted project and follow its instructions. Confirm the requested scope, installed skills, runtime tools, and any required workspace or approval. This selection includes the catalogued instruction requirements [`grilling`](/skills/grilling). Resolve them before use.

## Steps

1. **Request an interview.** [`grill-me`](/skills/grill-me) provides the guidance for this step.
2. **Pressure-test decisions.** [`grilling`](/skills/grilling) provides the guidance for this step.
3. **Check open assumptions.** [`grilling`](/skills/grilling) provides the guidance for this step.
4. **Summarize open decisions.** [`grilling`](/skills/grilling) provides the guidance for this step.

## Where to stop

Stop after the interview when the decision is clear, or research a disputed fact. The optional wait-what branch asks for an unclear reply to be explained again; it is not a design critic.

## Optional branches

- Choose [`research`](/skills/research) when a decision depends on facts, alternatives, feasibility, or evidence that you do not yet have.
- Choose [`specify`](/skills/specify) when the direction is agreed but observable behavior, acceptance criteria, constraints, or test boundaries remain unclear.
- Choose [`wait-what`](/skills/wait-what) when the last answer is hard to understand or omits context needed to follow the current proposal.

Optional branches are not prerequisites or permission to launch another phase. You can omit them or stop at the stated result. See [relationship types](/reference/relationships) for the distinction.
