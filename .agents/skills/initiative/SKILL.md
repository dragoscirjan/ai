# Initiative

Define the outcome, not an implementation plan.

Before generating the brief, resolve its destination and format from the target project's `AGENTS.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`. If absent or ambiguous, ask the user where to write it. Do not assume chat, a file, or an issue tracker.

1. Reuse research, existing artifacts, and confirmed answers. Use the required interview to resolve remaining decisions without repeating settled questions.
2. Capture the problem, intended outcome, observable success measures, scope, exclusions, constraints, assumptions, and open decisions.
3. Distinguish evidence from choices and assumptions. Reference supporting research instead of copying it.
4. Mark the brief as draft or agreed, with the evidence of agreement and the next authorized action.

Stop at the brief or unresolved decisions. Do not invent a technical solution to fill gaps, generate specifications automatically, or start implementation.

Follow project safety and approval gates. The interview does not grant permission to delegate, publish beyond the agreed destination, or execute the initiative.
